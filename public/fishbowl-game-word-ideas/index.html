How to build a good Fishbowl

The best prompt pool is mostly recognisable, with enough strange answers to create memorable clues. A useful split is roughly 60% easy, 30% medium and 10% wildcards. If every prompt is obscure, the first round becomes homework. If every prompt is obvious, the later rounds have nothing to build on.

Traditional Fishbowl vs CelebrityFishbowl often includes people, titles, objects and phrases. Celebrity-focused versions use famous people and characters. Celebrity Salad is intentionally in that celebrity-focused branch.

Which of these work in Celebrity Salad?

Celebrity Salad is built around famous people and recognisable characters rather than general objects or phrases. Use the first two sections as direct inspiration, or visit our 100 celebrity name ideas for a larger people-focused list.

If you are playing a broader paper Fishbowl, all five categories are fair game as long as the group agrees before the first round. For the rules and naming differences, see Salad Bowl and Fishbowl explained.
