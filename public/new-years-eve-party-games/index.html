NEW YEAR'S EVE 2026

Games worth playing before midnight.

Fast team games, music, trivia and low-prep ideas for the long stretch between everybody arriving and the countdown actually beginning.

12 New Year's Eve party games

1. Celebrity Salad

Players privately submit famous names and two to four teams guess the same pool across three rounds: Describe It, One Word and Act It. It works particularly well on New Year's Eve because the celebrity pool can mix people from the year just gone with long-standing favourites and ridiculous wildcards.

One shared iPhone or iPad keeps the timer and score. Four to twelve players are supported.

2. The year in headlines

Read short clues to major entertainment, sport or culture stories from the year and let teams identify the event or person. Keep politics and serious news optional depending on the room.

3. Music intros of the year

Use tracks that were actually part of your group's year rather than trying to create an objective chart exam. Mix recent songs with older tracks that came back into the culture.

4. Predictions for next year

Everybody writes three predictions: one plausible, one ambitious and one ridiculous. Seal or save them and revisit next New Year's Eve. The game is in comparing predictions before they are stored.

5. Who Am I?

Use celebrities and characters from the year. Players ask yes-or-no questions until they identify the name assigned to them.

6. Resolution bluff

Each person writes one real goal and one invented one. Read them anonymously and let the group guess which is genuine. Keep the goals light enough that nobody has to disclose something private.

7. Year-in-review categories

Pick categories such as films you watched, places people travelled, songs that were everywhere or things the group started doing this year. Go around without repeating answers.

8. Photo scavenger hunt

Teams collect harmless photos from the evening: something gold, someone wearing a hat, a clock, a group selfie, a snack nobody has eaten yet. Avoid tasks that involve photographing strangers without permission.

9. New Year's Charades

Use films, songs, celebrities and memorable moments from the year. Acting keeps the energy high when the party starts to dip.

10. Table trivia

Run four short rounds instead of one enormous quiz: music, film and TV, general knowledge and a “this year” round. Teams make it more social than individual answer sheets.

11. Best-of-the-year debate

Give teams deliberately subjective categories: best film, best snack discovery, best group chat moment, best song to hear at 1am. Award the point to the argument that wins the room.

12. Midnight bingo

Create a simple list of things likely to happen before midnight: somebody asks how long is left, a song gets restarted, somebody loses their drink, somebody starts filming too early. It can run quietly in the background.

A simple New Year's Eve games timeline

  1. While people arrive: Who Am I?, predictions or midnight bingo.
  2. Middle of the evening: Celebrity Salad, Charades or trivia.
  3. Last half-hour: music, year-in-review categories and anything that can stop instantly for the countdown.

New Year's Eve games do not need drinking rules

Time pressure, teams and shared references create enough energy on their own. Keeping alcohol separate from the mechanics makes the same game work for drivers, non-drinkers, teenagers, parents and anyone who simply wants to play without penalties.

Do not start a complicated game at 11:45

The closer you get to midnight, the shorter the format should become. Use categories, music, bingo or a quick final round. Save any game with setup, scoring or several stages for earlier in the evening.
