WHY IT WORKS

The clue changes. The names don’t.

That is the whole trick. A clue that barely worked in Round One becomes a shared joke by Round Three. Every turn adds context, memory and increasingly questionable acting.

01

Describe It

Say anything you like except the celebrity’s name. Fast clues, strange associations, accidental in-jokes.

02

One Word

The same celebrities return, but now you get one clue word. Memory starts doing most of the work.

03

Act It

No words. Just commit. By now your team should know the names well enough to decode almost anything.

THE RECIPE

One shared device. One room. No account required.

Celebrity Salad is deliberately a face-to-face game. The app handles the timer, teams, score and cards so the people in the room can focus on the guessing.

  1. 1
    Add your players

    Four or more works best. Nicknames are fine.

  2. 2
    Build the Salad

    Pass the iPhone or iPad around and add celebrity names privately.

  3. 3
    Split into two teams

    Shuffle automatically or adjust the teams yourself.

  4. 4
    Play all three rounds

    The app keeps time and score while the room does the rest.

QUICK ANSWERS

Celebrity Salad FAQs

What is Celebrity Salad?

Celebrity Salad is a team party game where players submit celebrity names and guess the same set across three rounds: Describe It, One Word and Act It.

How many people can play?

Four is a practical minimum because you need two teams. It scales well to larger groups by reducing submissions per player or shortening turns.

Is Celebrity Salad the same as Fishbowl or Salad Bowl?

They are closely related names for the same family of guessing games. Celebrity Salad focuses the format on famous people and runs the game on one shared Apple device.

Does everyone need their own phone?

No. One iPhone or iPad runs the game and is passed between players when names are submitted and when each turn begins.
